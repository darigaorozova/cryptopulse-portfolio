## Topic

A fictional neobank lets customers buy and sell crypto in its app in 6 EU countries
(DE, FR, ES, IT, NL, PL) and launches a pilot in Kyrgyzstan and Kazakhstan (KG, KZ) on 01.09.2026.
The warehouse collects trades, the user reference table, market prices and KYC statuses every day
and answers these questions:

1. How do activity and revenue in the pilot markets KG and KZ differ from the EU countries?
2. How does the daily movement of the Bitcoin price affect the number of trades and the volume?
3. How much does the bank earn from fees by day, country and plan?

Coins (CoinGecko ids): bitcoin, ethereum, solana, ripple, cardano, usd-coin.

## Fields

**trades:** `trade_id`, `trade_ts` (UTC), `user_id`, `coin_id`, `side` (buy / sell),
`amount_eur`, `fee_eur`, `status` (completed / failed / cancelled).

**users:** `user_id`, `country_code`, `signup_date`, `plan` (standard / plus / premium / metal / ultra),
`age_group`, `acquisition_channel`.

**users_kyc:** `user_id`, `kyc_status` (pending / verified / rejected), `updated_at`.

## Relationships

- `trades.user_id` → `users.user_id`
- `trades.coin_id` → CoinGecko coin id
- `users_kyc.user_id` → `users.user_id`

## Data origin

- Trades, users and KYC statuses are **synthetic**, generated by `data/generate_data.py`
  (fixed seed, so the result is reproducible).
- Fees are calibrated to Revolut's public price list: 1.49% (standard, plus), 0.99% (premium, metal),
  0.49% (ultra), minimum €0.99.
- Coin prices are **real**, from CoinGecko.
- The KG and KZ pilot starts on 01.09.2026, so users from these countries have no trades before that date.
- Defects are added on purpose to test the ODS layer and the quality checks:
  ~0.3% duplicate rows, ~0.5% dates in `DD.MM.YYYY HH:MI:SS` format, ~0.2% empty amounts.

## API limits

CoinGecko demo plan: 10,000 requests per month, 100 per minute, history for the last 365 days.
The daily load uses 6 requests (≈ 2% of the monthly quota); a 120-day backfill uses 720 requests (≈ 7%).
